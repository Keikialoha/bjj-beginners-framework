Master the Foundations: Core Positions

Good jiu-jitsu starts with great positioning—learn the four essentials below.

Closed Guard

Control your opponent from the bottom: break posture, establish grips, and set up sweeps.

Mount

Stay heavy and balanced on top; use chest-to-chest pressure and attack arms.

Side Control

Keep hips low, control the near-side arm, and flatten your opponent for control.

Back Mount

Maintain a seat-belt grip, control the hips, and look for chokes or escapes.
